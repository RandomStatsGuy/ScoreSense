Finding your way around

On a phone: tap the page name at the top to switch pages. League opens Trades, Vibes, Rosters, Rules and Insights.

On a computer: use the tabs and League menu. Before making a move, check that the league is Tessa's Touchdown Memorial League and the right team is selected.

01 · Your weekly job

Set your starters

My team → Lineup

  1. Check the week. Look for empty slots, injuries and BYE players. A bye means no game that week.
  2. Tap a position badge, such as WR1. Choose an eligible bench player and compare the two.
  3. Tap Start [player] at [slot]. That saves the change. Check that the new player appears among your starters.
Before kickoff. Both players must still be unlocked. Check Thursday players before Thursday night, then recheck the rest before their games.
See the Lineup screen

Prefer the locker room? That works too.

My team → Room

  1. On a phone: tap a bench jersey, then tap a lit starter locker. On a computer: drag a bench jersey onto a lit locker.
  2. The lineup saves right away. Use Undo if offered and both players are still unlocked.

02 · Game day

Follow your matchup

This Week shows your matchup, the league scoreboard, chat and standings. Review lineup takes you back to your team.

Open Game center for player-by-player action. Compare Starters, peek at Bench, or choose League to see every game.

What the numbers mean

Projected points are estimates. Only your starters earn matchup points; bench points do not count. Live scores refresh automatically and stay provisional until the week is finalized.

PPR means one point per catch, plus the other scoring in Rules. Projections → Weekly helps you compare player outlooks.

03 · Keep your roster strong

Find a replacement

Fantasy → Free agents

  1. Tap one of your players or roster spots near the top to compare replacements. Or use Pos and the search icon.
  2. Tap a player for details. Compare points per game with points per game, rather than season totals.
  3. Use Add when free agency is open. During waivers, submit a claim and follow the page's status and deadline.

A claim is a request, not a completed pickup. This league uses waiver priority. If a claim includes a conditional drop, that player is released only if the claim succeeds.

How adds work explains the current window. Comparing players does not drop anyone.

See the Free agents screen

Need a roster spot?

My team → Player details → choose the player → Drop player. This releases them from your team. Dropping is different from moving someone to your bench.

After an add or trade: check My team and set your starters. A new player is not automatically your preferred starter.

04 · Find a deal

Offer a trade

Page picker → League → Trades

  1. In Build, choose a trade partner.
  2. Use Send and Receive to pick players. Exchange shows the package together.
  3. Choose Review trade. Check who gets each player, then Propose trade when ready.

An offer does not move players by itself. Check Inbox for offers and responses. The teams must accept before the trade goes through.

Rosters lets you browse everyone's team before starting a conversation.

05 · The family rivalry

Make the league your own

Your team, your look

My team → Room → Edit look: change the photo, banner or room, then Save look. Open a locker to give a player a nickname.

Talk a little football

Chat on This Week or tap the chat bubble. Use @ to mention someone, add reactions, or choose Direct for a private conversation.

Give out the bragging rights

In Game center, expand Standings & weekly awards to vote. League → Insights keeps league history.

Trust your gut

League → Vibes: swipe left to rate lower, right to rate higher. Compare your gut with the model. Ratings alone do not change your starters.

When draft season returns

Use Draft for scheduling and picking, Strategy to rank targets, and Tools → Mock draft to practice.

06 · Keep it simple

Rules & a little help

League → Rules has scoring and roster limits. If something looks wrong, check your league, team and week, then ask the commissioner.
