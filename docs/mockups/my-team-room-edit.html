A · The room reacts to you

Lockers answer the drag

Pick up a bench jersey and the lockers that fit light up with the point change on their plate; lockers that don’t fit close their door, locked ones read Locked. Drop to save, undo in the status line. Bench jerseys that beat a starter carry a small amber “+5.8 over Pitts”. On phone the bench is a sticky rack: tap a jersey to pick it up, tap a lit locker to place it.

B · The room tells you

Coach’s notes

An amber note hangs under any locker where the bench has a better option: “Kelce +5.8”. Tap the note and the swap happens, with Undo. The scoreboard counts the notes and jumps to the first. Drag and tap-to-compare still work for everything else; the wall stays quiet where your lineup is already right.

What stays the same.

Approved room materials, jerseys, banner backdrop, nickname card, and the undisclosed hold-to-swing. The scoreboard adds a compact opponent total so a swap has stakes (toggle it off under States if you’d rather keep matchup facts in Game center). Amber marks a suggested sit/start; blue marks the drop target or next action only. Locked players are visibly unavailable; missing projections read “Projection unavailable”, never 0. Sleeper leagues preview and hand off with Set lineup in Sleeper. Lineup tab: approved picker, bench beside starters, Start best lineup review. Manage roster keeps contracts, cuts and the cap sheet.

No chart libraries or new artwork. Jerseys and faces are placeholders for the existing room assets and headshot helpers. The opponent total is the one element here that needs a product call: PRODUCT.md currently keeps matchup information in Game center.
