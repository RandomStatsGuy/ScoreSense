What to review

Desktop: side-by-side rosters, shared search and position filters, immediate selection feedback, a live impact rail, and a persistent package summary with one next action. The sample Cook–Wilson trade moves your cap room from $15 to $9 and your starter projection from 137.0 to 139.5.

Phone: Send / Receive / Impact switch between views of the same package. Selection persists across views. The package and review action stay above the product navigation. Review becomes a readable vertical receipt; no wide tables or horizontal roster scrolling.

Navigation: Build constructs a deal. Discover compares managers. Ideas provides specific packages. Inbox owns responses and status. The partner picker is a shortcut into the same roster comparison. Advanced terms keeps multi-team moves, cuts, and existing dead-cap transfers accessible.

Review: name every destination, display cap and roster checks beside Propose, and explain acceptance and execution. In a linked league, player moves happen in Sleeper; agreed local terms wait for unanimous acceptance and synchronized destinations. Newly synced moves still receive their separate cap review.

Existing foundation and additional work

CapabilityImplementation path after design approval
Builder, proposal checks, InboxReuse LeagueTrades.jsx, tradeBuilderHelpers.js, and existing validation/proposal endpoints. This pass changes the grouping and presentation.
Cap and positional countsReuse tradeProjection.js. Salary + existing dead cap = against cap. Current roster salary includes active expiring deals; it is different from draft-surviving committed salary.
Current-week starter impactReuse tradeWeekPreview.js for the viewer’s private before/after estimate. Missing projections need stricter coverage handling: its current numeric fallback can turn missing inputs into zero.
Ideas and partner needsReuse src/draft_hub/trade_insights.py and tradeIdeaHelpers.js. Needs refer to starter-thin positions; extra depth is not a need. No fairness percentage or acceptance probability.
Rest-of-season improvementAdditional package-level aggregation and coverage checks are needed. Choose total roster vs optimized starter outlook, account for remaining weeks/byes, and name that basis. The mock shows unavailable instead of an invented estimate.
Longer-term contract valueSalary, type, and years remaining already exist. A future-year package schedule must reuse the league’s extension/cap helpers and expiry rules before adding longer-term values or savings.
Multi-team, cuts, dead-cap transfersPreserve the current recipient, dead-cap provenance, validation, and atomic proposal flows. Their entrypoints are mocked here; their complete editors are not simulated.
