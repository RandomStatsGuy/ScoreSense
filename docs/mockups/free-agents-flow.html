ScoreSense · Free agents · The pool meets your roster

Every free agent tells you who they beat on your team. Then you act in one step.

All three keep the approved September 29 list: acquisition banner, Pos / Sort / Search, compact headshots, per-game pace, season median, floor–ceiling bar, and a bottom (phone) or right (desktop) sheet. All three add the same thing the Trades Face-off and the Room added: the consequence sits beside the pick. A free agent row says “+1.2 over Warren · FLEX” when he’d start, the sheet shows him versus the starter he pushes out, and the roster spot (open spot or drop) is chosen in the same step. They differ in where your roster lives. Use Preview for Bids, Claims, Open adds, Pre-draft, and Sleeper-hosted.

Second pass, more consequence and rhythm: a scoreboard reads your lineup per game, what the pending moves do to it (111.4 → 114.4, counting up), and the best upgrade left in the pool. Every range bar carries a tick where your starter sits; the part of the range above him is amber, so a glance down the list shows who actually beats your guy. The sheet is a duel: two headshots, the per-game change between them, the lineup line under it. Weak spots say how many free agents beat them. The best row is tagged. Headshots take a matte team tint. Rows arrive when the list changes and a save flashes teal once; all of it stops under reduced motion.

What stays the same.

Approved list and sheet. Adds follow the real acquisition calendar; the banner says what the window is and when it runs. Pick-draft leagues keep ordered claims and conditional drops with no money anywhere; auction leagues keep bids, cap checks, and the personal walk-away (amber when a bid is above it). Place bid stays the only auction primary. Missing forecasts read “Projection unavailable”, never 0. Pre-draft keeps stars and a disabled Add with the reason. Sleeper-hosted leagues compare here and hand off with Open in Sleeper. Teal means saved; amber marks a gain or a warning; blue marks the spot you are shopping for and the next action only.

Two product calls hide in C: the action leaves the sheet for the row (PRODUCT currently says outlook and acquisition share one sheet), and a bid saves on tap rather than after a confirm.
